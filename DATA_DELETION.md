# Data deletion requests for GreetSlate

**Effective date:** 30 September 2026  
**App:** GreetSlate (Android package `com.greetslate`)  
**Developer:** Manoj Pathak  
**Contact:** manojpathakiiita@gmail.com

GreetSlate has **no user accounts** and no sign-in. The developer does not store your name, photos, greeting text, or city label on the developer’s servers.

Use the steps below to delete information on your phone or to ask about data processed by service providers (such as analytics).

## Delete data on your phone

Most information never leaves your device. You can remove it yourself:

1. **Uninstall GreetSlate** — removes app storage, preferences, signature photo, and saved city text from the phone.
2. **Or clear app storage** — Android **Settings → Apps → GreetSlate → Storage → Clear storage** (wording may vary by device).
3. **Location label** — turn off **Include my location** in the app (Settings or signature options). That removes the saved city and region text from the app’s settings.
4. **Signature photo** — turn off the signature image in the app, or uninstall / clear storage as above.
5. **Cards you already shared or saved** — images in your gallery or in another app (WhatsApp, Messages, and so on) are under your control there. The developer cannot delete copies you sent elsewhere.

## Stop optional analytics

If **Share anonymous usage data** is on, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase.

- Turn it **off** in **Settings** in the app to stop new events.
- The developer does not receive your name with those events and cannot look them up by name.
- Data already sent is handled under Google’s retention and privacy policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy
